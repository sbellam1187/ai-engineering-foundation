# pov-optimize-ai-model-selection-0211: Optimize AI Model Selection

## Point of view
Our model strategy will begin with frontier and standard models, fine-tuning as needed to optimize, and pursuing custom large language models only when necessary due to cost or capability gaps. 4. AI Platform Enablement 4.1 Machine Learning Capabilities

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
