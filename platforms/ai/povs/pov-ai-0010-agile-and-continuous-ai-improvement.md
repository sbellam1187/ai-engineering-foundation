# pov-agile-and-continuous-ai-improvement-0209: Agile and Continuous AI Improvement

## Point of view
Our approach to AI development will be iterative and agile, allowing for flexibility, continuous learning, and adaptation to evolving needs. Progress will be monitored regularly, user feedback will be gathered, and models and strategies will be refined based on insights. 3.11 Build vs. Buy Strategic Discipline Our approach to AI services will apply the “build versus buy” principle, purchasing proven AI commodity services and building custom AI solutions where differentiation is strategic, such as operations decision support and agentic orchestration for irregular operations.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
