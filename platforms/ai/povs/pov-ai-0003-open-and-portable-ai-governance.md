# pov-open-and-portable-ai-governance-0202: Open and Portable AI Governance

## Point of view
We will use open and portable AI APIs, models, and frameworks, discouraging proprietary technology through a “Not the Droid” list, which will only allow exceptions through a formal process. 3.4 Standardize AI Patterns and Enable with Platforms Standards, patterns, and templates will be established and promoted to ensure consistency in development and deployment. Scaling will be achieved through the reuse of common components such as agents, automations, and innerSource contributions.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
