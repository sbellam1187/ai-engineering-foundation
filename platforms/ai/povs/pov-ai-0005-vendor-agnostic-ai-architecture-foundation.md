# pov-vendor-agnostic-ai-architecture-foundation-0204: Vendor-Agnostic AI Architecture and Platform Foundation

## Point of view
To accelerate AI enablement, we will leverage SaaS AI platforms as a foundation, strategically building AI extensions and integration components to meet American Airlines’ needs while maintaining a vendor-agnostic architecture to avoid platform lock-in. 3.6 AI-Ready Data Products Our AI-ready data products will be curated, secure, and governed, with semantic layers, validated APIs, lineage, and quality service-level agreements.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
