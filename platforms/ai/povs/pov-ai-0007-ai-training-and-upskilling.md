# pov-ai-training-and-upskilling-0206: AI Training and Upskilling

## Point of view
To close AI skill gaps, we will invest in training and developing AI-complementary skills essential for effective adoption. We will invest in an AI Developer Experimentation Environment to enable hands on learning and accelerated upskilling 3.8 AI Business Value Maximation We will identify and prioritize products and use cases with the highest AI business value potential, as outlined in the Business and Technology Context section.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
