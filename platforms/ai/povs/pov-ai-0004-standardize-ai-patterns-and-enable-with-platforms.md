# pov-standardize-ai-patterns-and-enable-with-platforms-0203: Standardize AI Patterns and Enable with Platforms

## Point of view
Standards, patterns, and templates will be established and promoted to ensure consistency in development and deployment. Scaling will be achieved through the reuse of common components such as agents, automations, and innerSource contributions. AI patterns, inventions, and innerSource components that deliver competitive advantage will be institutionalized and enabled via AA’s technology platforms. 3.5 Vendor-Agnostic AI Architecture and Platform Foundation

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
