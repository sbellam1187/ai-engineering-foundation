# pov-responsible-ai-monitoring-and-enforcement-0200: Rigorous Responsible AI Monitoring and Enforcement

## Point of view
Rigorous testing, validation, monitoring, guardrails, and content safety will be implemented across all AI solutions, with human-centered oversight to ensure alignment with responsible AI principles. 3.2 Formal AI Technology Governance All AI software and artifacts will be governed via AA’s formal technology governance process.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
