# pov-formal-ai-technology-governance-0201: Formal AI Technology Governance

## Point of view
All AI software and artifacts will be governed via AA’s formal technology governance process. 3.3 Open and Portable AI Governance We will use open and portable AI APIs, models, and frameworks, discouraging proprietary technology through a “Not the Droid” list, which will only allow exceptions through a formal process.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
