# pov-leverage-full-ai-spectrum-0208: Leverage Full AI-Spectrum Potential

## Point of view
We will leverage the full AI spectrum, including classification, prediction, optimization, copilots, agentic workflows, and retrieval-augmented generation, as detailed in the Business and Technology Context. 3.10 Agile and Continuous AI Improvement Our approach to AI development will be iterative and agile, allowing for flexibility, continuous learning, and adaptation to evolving needs. Progress will be monitored regularly, user feedback will be gathered, and models and strategies will be refined based on insights.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
