# pov-build-vs-buy-strategic-discipline-0210: Build vs Buy Strategic Discipline

## Point of view
Build vs Buy Strategic Discipline

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
