# pov-ai-ready-data-products-0205: AI-Ready Data Products

## Point of view
Our AI-ready data products will be curated, secure, and governed, with semantic layers, validated APIs, lineage, and quality service-level agreements. 3.7 AI Training and Upskilling To close AI skill gaps, we will invest in training and developing AI-complementary skills essential for effective adoption. We will invest in an AI Developer Experimentation Environment to enable hands on learning and accelerated upskilling

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
