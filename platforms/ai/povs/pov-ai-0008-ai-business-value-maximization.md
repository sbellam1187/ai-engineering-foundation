# pov-ai-business-value-maximization-0207: AI Business Value Maximation

## Point of view
We will identify and prioritize products and use cases with the highest AI business value potential, as outlined in the Business and Technology Context section. 3.9 Leverage Full AI-Spectrum Potential We will leverage the full AI spectrum, including classification, prediction, optimization, copilots, agentic workflows, and retrieval-augmented generation, as detailed in the Business and Technology Context.

## Rationale
- Standardized governance and guardrails reduce risk.
- Platform-based patterns reduce time-to-production and operational variance.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Strong guardrails can introduce additional engineering and governance overhead.
