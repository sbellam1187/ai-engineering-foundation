# principle-scale-ai-with-platforms-0202: Scale AI with platforms

## Statement
We will accelerate value creation through a foundational AI platform that supports machine learning and generative AI capabilities at scale. AI support will be integrated into developer, runtime, telemetry, and integration platforms to enable end-to-end adoption. 2.4 Enable AI with Data We will enable AI solutions by building curated, secure, enterprise-grade data products—both operational and analytical—while ensuring data quality and integrity through semantic layers and validated APIs.

## Implications
- Standard governance applies to models, prompts, agents, pipelines, and enabling services.
- Platform-provided templates and guardrails are the preferred path for delivery.
- Exceptions MUST be documented and time-bounded.

## Measures of success
- Improved safety, auditability, and delivery velocity via standardized patterns.
