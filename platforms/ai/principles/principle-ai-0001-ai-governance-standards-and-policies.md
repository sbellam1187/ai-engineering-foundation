# principle-ai-governance-standards-and-policies-0200: AI governance, standards, and policies

## Statement
AI governance and security are paramount to our strategy. All AI models, artifacts, services, and platforms will adhere to the standard American Airlines technology governance process and comply with cybersecurity policies. We will integrate AI into processes and products to drive innovation, efficiency, and improved outcomes for both customers and operations. While we value standardization, we will periodically evaluate best-of-breed offerings in the marketplace to maintain strategic flexibility and maximize value. 2.2 Responsible AI

## Implications
- Standard governance applies to models, prompts, agents, pipelines, and enabling services.
- Platform-provided templates and guardrails are the preferred path for delivery.
- Exceptions MUST be documented and time-bounded.

## Measures of success
- Improved safety, auditability, and delivery velocity via standardized patterns.
