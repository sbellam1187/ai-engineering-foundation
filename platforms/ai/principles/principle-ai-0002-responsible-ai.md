# principle-responsible-ai-0201: Responsible AI

## Statement
Business and technology context centers on ROI, innovation, experience, reliability, and a full spectrum of AI capabilities—from classification and forecasting to agentic workflows and RAG—targeted at measurable outcomes including personalization, productivity, process optimization, and decision support. 1. Vision & Objectives Our vision is to become an airline that excels in building unrivaled digital products. To achieve this, we will enable AI in all aspects of our engineering lifecycle.

## Implications
- Standard governance applies to models, prompts, agents, pipelines, and enabling services.
- Platform-provided templates and guardrails are the preferred path for delivery.
- Exceptions MUST be documented and time-bounded.

## Measures of success
- Improved safety, auditability, and delivery velocity via standardized patterns.
