# principle-enable-ai-with-data-0203: Enable AI with data

## Statement
We will enable AI solutions by building curated, secure, enterprise-grade data products—both operational and analytical—while ensuring data quality and integrity through semantic layers and validated APIs. 2.5 Pragmatic Build vs. Buy Philosophy Our approach to AI services will follow the principle of “build versus buy” purchasing commodity AI services while building differentiated capabilities where they provide strategic advantage.

## Implications
- Standard governance applies to models, prompts, agents, pipelines, and enabling services.
- Platform-provided templates and guardrails are the preferred path for delivery.
- Exceptions MUST be documented and time-bounded.

## Measures of success
- Improved safety, auditability, and delivery velocity via standardized patterns.
