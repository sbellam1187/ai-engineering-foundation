# pov-rearchitect-over-lift-and-shift-0100: Re-architect and re-platform over lift-and-shift

## Point of view
Lift-and-shift provides speed at the cost of efficiency, resilience, and agility; re-architecting or re-platforming unlocks the value of cloud-native capabilities.

## Rationale
- Cloud-native designs improve scalability and resiliency while reducing long-term operating cost.
- Managed services and automation reduce operational toil and accelerate delivery.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Up-front engineering investment is higher than lift-and-shift.
- Legacy constraints may require phased modernization and exception handling.
