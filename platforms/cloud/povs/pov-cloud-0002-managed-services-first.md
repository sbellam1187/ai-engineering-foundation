# pov-managed-services-first-0101: Prefer managed services over IaaS

## Point of view
Prefer managed cloud services to reduce operational burden and accelerate delivery; use VM/IaaS only when justified.

## Rationale
- Managed services reduce patching and maintenance and typically provide stronger built-in reliability.
- Standardization improves skills reuse and governance consistency.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Vendor roadmap dependencies and potential coupling.
- Specialized performance or licensing needs may require IaaS.
