# pov-open-standards-with-pragmatic-native-0102: Prefer open standards; use open-aligned native services pragmatically

## Point of view
Open standards maximize portability and interoperability; leverage native services that align with open standards while avoiding non-essential proprietary services.

## Rationale
- Portability preserves leverage and reduces strategic risk.
- Native services can accelerate delivery when they do not create hard coupling or can be abstracted.

## Tradeoffs acknowledged
- Cross-cloud solutions may lag proprietary feature sets.
- Exceptions may be justified for differentiated capabilities or DR requirements.
